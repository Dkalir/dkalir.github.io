Teaching

I am passionate about education and creating a welcoming classroom. My pedagogy is built on the premise that knowledge is best retained when students have an active role in the classroom. I strive to create a fun, friendly environment where students can collaborate to develop both their critical thinking skills and their technical communication skills.
